/ How it plays

Three core mechanics.

01

Collect

Discover athletes by swipe, search, or quiz reward. Every card you find joins your dex — sortable by sport, country, era, or tier.

02

Battle

Pit two athletes head-to-head on raw stats. Goals vs goals, titles vs titles, era vs era. Settle bar-room arguments with data.

03

Level up

Rarer cards earn more XP. Quizzes earn badges. Your collection grade rises as your knowledge does.
